# DAC6 Hallmarks Overview

DAC6 hallmarks are indicators that a cross-border arrangement may be reportable under Mandatory Disclosure Rules.

Category C hallmarks commonly involve deductible cross-border payments, including cases where the recipient benefits from no or almost no taxation.

Category E hallmarks focus on transfer pricing and value shifts, including arrangements that materially reduce projected earnings before interest and tax.

Analysts should capture which hallmark code applies, why it applies, and the factual trigger found in the source material.
