# Case 2025-0042 — Acme Logistics v. Northwind Freight (Contract Dispute)

## Case Summary
Case Number: 2025-0042
Matter Type: Commercial Contract Dispute
Status: Active — Discovery
Jurisdiction: State of Washington, King County Superior Court
Date Opened: 2025-03-14
Assigned Attorney: J. Rivera

Acme Logistics ("Plaintiff") alleges that Northwind Freight ("Defendant") breached a
three-year master services agreement by failing to deliver contracted freight capacity
during Q4 2024, resulting in $1.2M of cover damages.

## Guidance on Handling a Contract Dispute Case
When handling a contract dispute, follow these established steps:

1. **Review the contract terms.** Identify the clauses governing breach, dispute
   resolution, limitation of liability, and governing law. For Case 2025-0042 the
   controlling document is the Master Services Agreement dated 2023-01-01, Sections 7
   (Service Levels) and 12 (Dispute Resolution).
2. **Preserve and document evidence.** Collect all relevant communications, purchase
   orders, delivery logs, and invoices. Issue a litigation hold to all custodians.
3. **Exhaust contractual dispute-resolution mechanisms first.** Section 12 requires the
   parties to attempt good-faith mediation before filing suit. Confirm mediation was
   demanded and document the outcome.
4. **Assess the strength of the claim with counsel.** Evaluate breach elements,
   causation, and the quantum of damages (here, cover damages under UCC 2-712).
5. **Send a formal demand letter** outlining the breach and the remedy sought before
   escalating to litigation.

## Key Dates
- 2024-10-01: Alleged service-level failures begin.
- 2025-02-10: Mediation demanded under Section 12; mediation held 2025-02-28 (no resolution).
- 2025-03-14: Complaint filed.

## Recommended Remedy
Pursue expectation damages plus cover damages under UCC 2-712, subject to the
$2.0M liability cap in Section 7.4 of the Master Services Agreement.
