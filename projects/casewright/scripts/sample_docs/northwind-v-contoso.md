# Case File: Northwind Logistics v. Contoso Freight — Breach of Contract

**Case Number:** CW-2024-0182
**Jurisdiction:** Superior Court of Washington, King County
**Status:** Active — Discovery Phase
**Lead Counsel:** Dana Okafor (Northwind, Plaintiff)

## Summary

Northwind Logistics ("Northwind") filed suit against Contoso Freight ("Contoso")
alleging breach of a multi-year freight services agreement signed on March 14, 2022.
Northwind claims Contoso failed to deliver 38 scheduled shipments between June and
November 2023, resulting in $1.2M in documented losses and reputational harm.

## Key Facts

- The Master Services Agreement (MSA) required Contoso to maintain a 98% on-time
  delivery rate measured monthly.
- Contoso's on-time rate fell to 71% in Q3 2023, below the contractual threshold.
- Section 7.2 of the MSA specifies liquidated damages of $5,000 per missed shipment.
- Contoso asserts a force majeure defense citing regional port closures in
  September 2023.

## Claims

1. **Breach of Contract** — failure to meet the on-time delivery SLA in Section 4.1.
2. **Liquidated Damages** — $190,000 across 38 missed shipments under Section 7.2.
3. **Consequential Damages** — $1.01M in lost downstream contracts.

## Contoso's Defenses

- Force majeure under Section 12.3 (port closures and labor strikes).
- Northwind's alleged failure to provide accurate shipment forecasts as required by
  Section 5.4, which Contoso argues caused the delays.

## Discovery Notes

- Plaintiff requests all port-closure notices and carrier logs from August–October 2023.
- Defendant requests Northwind's internal forecasting models and email correspondence.
- Mediation is tentatively scheduled for the first week of February 2025.

## Next Steps

- Complete document production by January 17, 2025.
- Depose Contoso's operations director regarding the Q3 delivery shortfall.
- Evaluate the strength of the force majeure defense against the MSA's notice
  requirements in Section 12.3(b).
