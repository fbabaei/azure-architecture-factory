# Blue Detection Generator AgentPack certification

## Status

`certification-ready`

## Summary

Drafts detection logic, incident triage steps, and XDR/SIEM evidence mappings from validated attack paths.

## Certification evidence

- Detection and triage evidence are represented by `blue-detection-draft`.
- Approved telemetry-source boundaries are defined in `tool-integrations.json`.
- Human review before production enablement is required by the approval state and approval workflows.
- Containment actions remain approval-gated.

## Certification note

This AgentPack is certification-ready for controlled preview. It can draft and recommend detections, but production enablement and containment must remain human-approved.
